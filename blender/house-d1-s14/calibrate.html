Scale calibration

Walk through the ground floor and tune the view until it feels like standing in it. Save the settings and the finished model will use them.

  1. Set your height. The camera starts at your eye level.
  2. Walk into a room you can picture, like the kitchen. Change Scale and Camera height until it feels like the real thing. While walking, , and . move the walls closer or further apart, and R and F move the camera down or up.
  3. Walk at your normal pace and match the speed to it.
  4. Save when it feels right.
